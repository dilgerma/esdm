# Concepts

An Event Modeling blueprint is told on a timeline, and the extension has one concept for each thing you point at on the wall. Each concept is a document kind of its own, and none of them repeats what the core already says: they refer to Commands, Events, Read Models, and the rest **by name**.

This chapter walks through each concept on its own page:

- An **[Event Model](/extensions/event-modeling/concepts/event-model.md)** is the timeline: its chapters, left to right, each listing its slices in order.
- A **[Slice](/extensions/event-modeling/concepts/slice.md)** is one vertical cut through the blueprint, of one of four types, made of exactly the parts its type needs.
- A **[Screen](/extensions/event-modeling/concepts/screen.md)** is what the user sees and does: the Read Models it shows and the Commands it triggers.
